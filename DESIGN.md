# Adverse Media Review Desk design system

This is an analyst workstation, not a marketing dashboard. It should feel like a precise evidence-comparison surface used during customer onboarding and quality control.

## Direction

- Light neutral canvas for sustained investigation work.
- Navy ink for primary content, cobalt for actions and selection, amber only for unresolved evidence.
- Public Sans for dense operational copy and tabular information.
- Borders and spacing establish hierarchy. Avoid decorative gradients, glass effects and stacked cards.
- Bank data and online-source data stay visually distinct while retaining equal evidentiary weight.

## Interaction rules

- Minimum 44px touch target for controls and visible keyboard focus.
- Match, conflict, variant and unknown states never rely on color alone.
- Missing information remains unknown and is never silently converted to a mismatch.
- Analyst and QC decisions remain separate and reviewable.

## Layout

- Desktop: stable case navigation beside a wide comparison workspace.
- Mobile: a single-column workflow with scrollable evidence tables where necessary.
- Explanatory copy stays within a comfortable reading measure.
